# Online Resource 1: Primary-study evidence matrix

Companion to *From Checked Artefacts to System Assurance: A Critical Review of Trust Boundaries in Generative AI and Formal Methods*. Prepared 23 September 2026. This is a purposive comparison of 20 studies or study families, not an exhaustive screen or a frequency estimate. Eighteen entries came from the retained full-text comparison register; Astrogator and Alloy tests were added by targeted title/topic search. ACCESS and the continuous-assurance CLARISSA study are contextual, non-generative or mixed precedents. A publication and its preprint are counted once. Locations point to the linked version; claims are attributed to the papers unless this review independently checked them. The authors should confirm each interpretation against the final submission versions.

The four appraisal columns ask what evidence a *broader deployed-system claim* would need: **S** specification adequacy, **M** semantic correspondence between representations, **O** mediation of live observations and effects, and **C** composition of results under environmental assumptions. **E** means the study explicitly supplies evidence for that link within a stated scope; **P** means a bounded test, review or partial relation is supplied; **R** means a broader reuse claim relies on this link as an assumption or external input; **–** means the link is outside the study's declared objective. These are this review's claim-specific appraisals, not ratings of paper quality. A dash is not a failure. Even E is not a universal guarantee.

Role codes: A generate/check; B generate/prove; C model-based or empirical assessment; D formalism serving the model; E runtime enforcement; F assurance-oriented. Codes may overlap and do not change the one-label arXiv map.

## Cross-study inferences and limits

Specification validation is an active research area: Astrogator proposes query review, Lahiri et al. and the Alloy tests probe candidate specifications, and Herald reviews a selected set of formal translations. None makes all user intent machine-checkable. Offline/online coupling is also real: VeriGuard checks a policy before deploying an interceptor, AESOP combines slow advice with fast recovery control, and ToolGate constrains symbolic state changes. Their live data mapping, latency and external-effect assumptions differ. ACCESS, CAF and both CLARISSA studies already connect or maintain assurance evidence; the question for a given system is the truth and completeness of the represented links. These observations follow from purposive cases and cannot establish how often any link is addressed across the field.

## Reproducibility and provenance index

Online Resource 2 is the companion ZIP with the saved `corpus_labelled.json`, `lit_search.py`, `label_corrections.json`, `check_counts.py`, the corpus note, and the underlying comparison register. It preserves the 363-record arXiv denominator plus two tagged supplemental records; the counting script excludes the latter. `lit_search.py` records the fourteen historical query families and original classification prompt. The original retrieval timestamp, raw query responses, exclusion log, classifier version/settings and per-record rationales were not retained. The regular-expression repair and two targeted corrections do not restore that history or constitute independent human validation. The original targeted matrix is also in the ZIP as an audit record; the English extraction above is the reader-facing submission version.
